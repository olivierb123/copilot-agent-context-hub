# Expanded Synthetic Sales Demo

Dataset `synthetic-iq-sales-v1` is authored demonstration data, not live CRM, actual customer evidence, independently verified product documentation or a current-market claim. Its fixed simulated as-of date is **2026-10-30**, not verified source freshness. Original records, fiscal labels and dates are preserved.

## Contents

| Data | Count | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Territories | 6 | West, Central, South, Northeast, Southeast, Northwest |
| Sales performance | 72 rows | Six closed months, two segments per territory |
| Fictional accounts | 18 | Three per territory, synthetic names and owners |
| Opportunities | 36 | Two per account; 24 open, 6 Closed Won, 6 Closed Lost |
| Capability scenarios | 6 | Availability, preview, validation and roadmap distinctions |
| New knowledge-base documents | 12 | Discovery, handoff, rollout, security, competitive evaluation, forecast rules and provenance |

Opportunity records include account/owner, stage, amount, probability, expected close date, last activity, buyer need, blocker, next action, capability and supporting document ID. These are authored scenarios, not real buyer commitments. Probabilities are planning assumptions, not calibrated forecasts. Weather-aware scheduling is explicitly roadmap-only, not available.

The [complete dataset](../data/synthetic-iq-sales-v1/dataset.json), [hashed manifest](../data/synthetic-iq-sales-v1/manifest.json), [sales CSV](../data/synthetic-iq-sales-v1/sales_performance.csv), [accounts CSV](../data/synthetic-iq-sales-v1/accounts.csv), [opportunities CSV](../data/synthetic-iq-sales-v1/opportunities.csv), and [Search documents](../data/synthetic-iq-sales-v1/search-documents.json) are generated by [the deterministic generator](../scripts/generate_iq_demo_data.mjs). No source credentials are needed to generate them.

## Periods And Relationships

- April-June 2026 sales: FY26/Q4. July-September 2026 sales: FY27/Q1.
- October-December 2026 opportunities: FY27/Q2. Pipeline is not booked sales.
- The original five-row FieldForge FY26/Q1 labels remain unchanged. Do not reinterpret them using the new pack's convention or combine both models' totals.
- Sales grain is month/territory/segment. SUM revenue and quota before calculating attainment; never average row percentages.
- Opportunities join to accounts by accountId. Accounts and sales link to the shared territories dimension by territoryId. Opportunities also link to capabilities.
- Aggregate sales and opportunity values independently before comparing them. Do not multiply quota by joining sales facts to multiple accounts/opportunities. There is no account-level recognized-revenue allocation.

Each row carries datasetId, classification, simulatedAsOf and provenance. Document IDs use `SYN-IQ-V1-DOC-01` through `SYN-IQ-V1-DOC-12`; every document explicitly discloses its synthetic origin. `supportingDocId` is a reference, not proof that the document was retrieved or that its claims are verified.
